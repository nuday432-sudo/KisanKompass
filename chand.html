🍃 Organic Fertilizer Guide

🌿 Compost Manure

Materials: Dry leaves, crop residues, animal manure and water.

Method: Layer the materials, maintain moisture, and allow them to decompose before using.

🍀 Neem-Based Pest Repellent

Materials: Neem seed kernel extract and water.

Method: Prepare a properly filtered neem extract and use an agriculture-approved application rate.

🌱 Vermicompost

Materials: Partially decomposed organic matter and suitable earthworms.

Method: Maintain a shaded, moist bed and allow earthworms to convert organic waste into manure.

Demo educational recipes. Confirm crop suitability and application rates with a local agricultural officer.
